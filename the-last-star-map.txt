The Last Star Map

In the year 2187, twelve-year-old Nova lived aboard Aurora Station, a research colony floating above the blue planet Kepler-91.

Nova loved exploring the station's forgotten storage rooms. Her best friend was Eli, a young engineering apprentice who could repair almost anything with a circuit board and a few pieces of wire.

One evening, while searching through an abandoned observatory, Nova discovered a small metal box hidden beneath an old telescope. Inside was a transparent star map covered with glowing blue symbols.

When Eli connected the map to a portable power cell, a bright hologram appeared above their hands. It showed a route leading far beyond the known navigation network.

At the end of the route was a tiny golden star marked with a message:

"When the last light disappears, follow the silent moon."

Nova believed the map had belonged to the first explorers of Kepler-91. Eli was less certain, but his curiosity quickly overcame his caution.

The two friends secretly prepared a small exploration shuttle.

Nova wore her white exploration jacket with orange markings and carried the glowing star map inside a protective case. Eli packed his engineering tools, a compact scanner, and enough emergency supplies for several days.

Before sunrise, their shuttle left Aurora Station.

They followed the mysterious route through a field of enormous blue crystals drifting silently through space. Beyond the crystals, they discovered a small moon hidden in the shadow of a gas giant.

There were no cities on its surface and no signals from any spacecraft.

Only a single ancient tower stood in the middle of a silver desert.

Nova and Eli landed nearby.

As they approached the tower, the star map began glowing brighter.

A circular door slowly opened.

Inside, thousands of tiny lights appeared across the walls like artificial stars. At the center of the chamber stood an enormous mechanical model of the galaxy.

Nova placed the star map into an empty slot in the machine.

The entire chamber awakened.

A golden path appeared across the model, connecting dozens of worlds that were missing from every modern star chart.

Eli stared at the glowing galaxy in amazement.

They had not discovered treasure.

They had discovered a forgotten map created by explorers hundreds of years before them.

Nova carefully removed the star map and looked toward the open doorway, where the distant planet Kepler-91 filled the sky.

Their journey had only revealed the first destination.

There were still dozens of forgotten worlds waiting beyond the stars.
